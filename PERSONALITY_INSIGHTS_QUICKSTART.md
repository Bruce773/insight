## The Big Five Traits

### 🧠 Neuroticism
How much you experience negative emotions (anxiety, sadness, irritability)
- **Low**: Calm, resilient, handles pressure well
- **High**: Sensitive to emotions, vigilant, naturally empathetic

### 🌟 Extraversion  
How much you seek social interaction and stimulation
- **Low**: Prefers quiet, focused work, one-on-one interaction
- **High**: Energized by groups, outgoing, loves collaboration

### 💡 Openness to Experience
How curious and creative you are
- **Low**: Practical, prefers proven methods, values tradition
- **High**: Intellectually curious, creative, embraces change

### 🤝 Agreeableness
How much you prioritize cooperation and others' well-being
- **Low**: Direct, assertive, comfortable with competition
- **High**: Compassionate, cooperative, team-focused

### 📋 Conscientiousness
How organized and committed to goals you are (strongest job performance predictor)
- **Low**: Flexible, spontaneous, adaptable to change
- **High**: Very organized, detail-oriented, reliable

## How Your Scores Are Calculated

Your scores range from 0.0 to 1.0:
- **0.0–0.33**: Notably low
- **0.33–0.60**: Low to moderate
- **0.40–0.60**: Moderate
- **0.60–0.75**: Moderate to high
- **0.75–1.0**: Notably high

## Important Things to Know

### These scores measure *tendencies*, not *abilities*
You can develop skills and behaviors outside your natural preferences. High extraversion doesn't mean you *can't* focus deeply; it means you're more naturally energized by social interaction.

### Every personality profile has strengths
There's no "best" personality for success. Different roles benefit from different personality profiles. The goal is self-awareness, not evaluation.

### Your answers reflect this moment
How you respond depends on context. You might be more extraverted at work than at home, or more conscientious when stressed.

### These scores don't predict job fit
Personality is one factor among many (skills, experience, training, interests). Success depends on fit between your personality, the role demands, and your motivation.
